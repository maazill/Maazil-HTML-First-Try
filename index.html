My Favorite Anime

1. Naruto

Naruto is a Japanese manga series created by Masashi Kishimoto.
It tells the story of Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who seeks to gain recognition from his peers and also dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village.

2. One Piece

One Piece is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Eiichiro Oda.
It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew in order to find the greatest treasure ever left by the legendary Pirate, Gold Roger.
The famous mystery treasure named "One Piece".

3. Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama
